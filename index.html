The Problem

Lithium-ion battery fires are becoming increasingly common. In 2024, over 15,000 EV battery fires were reported in the United States—a 40% year-over-year increase.

Current industry response lacks standardized, compliant containment solutions. The result: thermal runaway events spread uncontrolled, environmental contamination occurs, and first responders lack clear protocols.

There is no industry standard for safe, compliant battery fire containment.

Our Solution: EVault

EVault is an open-top, watertight containment system engineered for on-scene suppression and environmental protection during lithium-ion battery fire incidents.

✓ Suppression

Immersion in suppression medium prevents thermal runaway and secondary ignitions.

✓ Containment

Watertight design captures all hazardous effluent, preventing environmental contamination.

✓ Compliance

Aligns with California AB 841, NFPA 1006, and PHMSA hazmat transportation standards.

✓ Rapid Deployment

Integrates with existing tow and emergency response infrastructure.
