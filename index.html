The Problem

EV Battery Fires Are a Growing Crisis With No Complete Solution

When a lithium-ion battery enters thermal runaway, it doesn't just burn — it releases hydrogen fluoride (HF), a highly toxic acid that contaminates water, injures first responders, and creates significant environmental liability.

15 kg HF potential per 75 kWh pack
Larsson et al., 2017 — Scientific Reports
  • Standard water suppression controls flames but cannot stop thermal runaway propagation or reignition
  • Toxic HF runoff contaminates storm drains, soil, and waterways — RCRA hazardous waste violation
  • Reignition during tow operations has injured and disabled career firefighters (Sacramento FD, April 2025)
  • USFA/FEMA: "No generally accepted, scientifically validated method" exists for EV battery fire suppression
  • Every current containment product captures runoff but sends it to hazmat disposal — no competitor treats it on-scene
📋 Field Incident — Sacramento Fire Department, April 11, 2025

A Tesla Model S struck a tree and went into thermal runaway. Fire crews suppressed visible flames and declared the scene controlled. As the tow truck began moving the vehicle, the battery reignited — releasing a toxic vapor plume of HF and metal nanoparticles. Five firefighters were exposed. Four remain unable to return to work as of October 2025, with injuries including 20% lung function decrease, renal compromise, and cardiac symptoms. NIOSH conducted a formal federal investigation. This is the exact operational gap EVault is designed to close. (Source: Sacramento FD Green Sheet, CA Energy Commission TN 268919; NIOSH 2026)

Our Solution

Four Pillars. One Deployment. Nothing Left Behind.

EVault is an open-top, watertight roll-off container platform that covers the complete EV battery fire incident lifecycle — from first flame to final disposal.

🔥

Thermal Suppression

Full vehicle immersion eliminates thermal runaway propagation. Direct liquid contact increases heat transfer 1,000–10,000× vs. air cooling. Open-top design vents hydrogen gas safely, preventing secondary explosions.

⚗️

HF Neutralization

Pre-loaded potassium acetate (KAc) neutralizes hydrogen fluoride as it evolves. Calcium carbonate precipitates dissolved fluoride as calcium fluoride (CaF₂) — a non-hazardous insoluble solid. No competitor does this.

🛡️

RCRA-Compliant Runoff Capture

Watertight walls and floor contain 100% of incident water. Chemically treated water becomes containerized waste — significantly reduced acute toxicity before transport to licensed TSDF.

Transport-Safe Storage

Submerged vehicles can be safely transported to long-term storage — eliminating reignition risk during towing. Designed for hook-lift roll-off trucks already in every tow fleet.

The Science

In-Situ HF Neutralization Chemistry

EVault's two-step chemistry converts toxic hydrogen fluoride into a non-hazardous solid during the suppression event itself — a first in the industry.

Step 1

HF Neutralization

KAc + HF → KF + CH₃COOH

Potassium acetate reacts with hydrogen fluoride via established acid-base chemistry. Equilibrium strongly favors reaction to completion (Ka HF = 6.8×10⁻⁴ vs. Ka acetic acid = 1.8×10⁻⁵).

Step 2

Fluoride Precipitation

CaCO₃ + 2F⁻ → CaF₂↓ + CO₃²⁻

Calcium carbonate (activated by dilute HCl) precipitates dissolved fluoride as calcium fluoride — a naturally occurring, non-hazardous mineral. Wang et al. (2019) confirmed 96.2% fluoride removal.

Result

Non-Hazardous Solid

CaF₂ (Ksp = 3.9×10⁻¹¹)

Calcium fluoride (fluorspar) is essentially insoluble, not RCRA-listed, and naturally occurring. The captured water is significantly detoxified before transport — reducing disposal cost and liability.
